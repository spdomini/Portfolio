SOPHIA DOMINICIS
About

Sophia is a dual-degree student at Syracuse University, pursuing a B.S. in Broadcast & Digital Journalism through the S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications alongside a B.S. in Supply Chain Management through the Whitman School of Management.

She reports and produces for CitrusTV and NCC News, and has held newsroom, operations, and communications roles at Amazon, La Casita Cultural Center, and Girl Scouts of Eastern Missouri. She currently serves as Treasurer of Syracuse's chapter of the National Association of Hispanic Journalists.
